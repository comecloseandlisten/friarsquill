Inquisition prompts - quick guide

Use the four fields in the sidebar to steer the verdict. Works for any content: calls, videos, lectures, propaganda analysis, etc.

  • Evaluation goal: Describe what you want to evaluate. This sets the overall direction of the analysis.
  • What to look for: One item per line. Key elements, claims, or markers that should be found and tracked.
  • Red flags / warnings: One item per line. Problematic patterns, manipulative techniques, or violations to flag.
  • Scoring criteria: One criterion per line. Each criterion will get an individual X/10 score with justification.
  • Keep prompts concrete: Prefer "uses emotional manipulation without evidence" over "bad video".
  • Stay language-aware: Write prompts in the output language you expect from the report.
Evaluation goal:
Evaluate how propagandistic this video is

What to look for:
- factual claims with sources
- balanced presentation of viewpoints
- clear attribution of opinions

Red flags / warnings:
- emotional manipulation without evidence
- dehumanizing language
- false dichotomies
- cherry-picked statistics

Scoring criteria:
- objectivity
- factual accuracy
- source quality
- balanced representation
