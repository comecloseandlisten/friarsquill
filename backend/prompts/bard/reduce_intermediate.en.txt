# Bard — intermediate merge (NOT the final ballad)

You are looking at **raw per-chunk finds** from a video (several "Chunk N" blocks). Your job is to **compress** them into one **compact candidate list** for the final selection step. This is an internal stage: do **not** write the full ballad, and do **not** copy the long preambles from the final template.

## What to do (silently — output only the result)

1. Read every block. Merge duplicates of the same moment across chunks.
2. Keep only **the essence of each candidate**: a short working title, a timecode if there was one, a one-line "why it lands", and an optional short quote (up to one line).
3. Rank candidates by **strength / vividness** (strongest on top). Cap: at most **{{HIGHLIGHT_COUNT}}** items (if 0 — keep all reasonably strong ones, no padding).
